About

Who I am and where I'm headed.

I came to cybersecurity through intrusion detection: how a system decides something is an attack, and what it costs when it's wrong. At Kanto Gakuin University I spent a year and a half benchmarking machine learning models on the CICIDS2017 dataset. I then moved to the US to study cyber defense and digital forensics at Edmonds College, and I'm now earning a master's at Central Washington University. I'm working toward a role as a cybersecurity solutions engineer or a SOC analyst: turning what I know about detection and real-world threats into security solutions that hold up in practice.
